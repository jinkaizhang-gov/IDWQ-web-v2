Annual Water Quality Index values have been calculated for each site using the Canadian Council of Ministers of the Environment (CCME) Water Quality Index that compares site specific water quality data to irrigation water quality guidelines that were developed to protect all life stages of crop species grown in Canada. Irrigation guidelines used for this index are for parameters monitored over the entire study period and are listed at the bottom of this page.

Water Quality Index values are a numerical output between 0 and 100 and are calculated based on:

  • How many guidelines are exceeded
  • How often guidelines are exceeded
  • How much guidelines are exceeded by

The rating system of water quality index values is:

Excellent (95–100)
Good (80–94.9)
Fair (65–79.9)
Marginal (45–64.9)
Poor (0–44.9)
