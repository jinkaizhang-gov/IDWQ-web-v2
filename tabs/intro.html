Irrigation District Water Quality Data Tool

This website provides access to water quality data collected throughout Alberta’s 12 irrigation districts as part of the Irrigation District Water Quality Project.

If you need more information or assistance, please contact info@albertairrigation.ca.

This project is a partnership between Alberta’s Irrigation Districts, Alberta Agriculture and Irrigation and Agriculture and Agri-food Canada to assess the quality of water in Alberta’s Irrigation Districts.

Funding for this project has been provided by Canada Alberta Water Supply Expansion Program, Alberta’s Irrigation Rehabilitation Program and Alberta Irrigation Districts.

Four different types of sites are monitored:

  • AEP – sites on government owned infrastructure where water is diverted from a river and delivered to an irrigation district
  • Primary – sites on main canals where source water enters an irrigation district (site name denoted by "-P")
  • Secondary – sites on lateral canals that branch off of main canals or are immediately downstream of mid-district reservoirs (site name denoted by "-S")
  • Return - sites at the end of irrigation district infrastructure where the unused irrigation water returns to the rivers (site name denoted by "-R")
